# Open Research Problems

## Overview

This document summarizes major unresolved research problems within the MDST and Yupana CRT framework.

The framework remains exploratory, and many foundational mathematical and computational questions are still open.

---

# 1. Basin Classification Theory

The operator:

\[
\Phi(x)
\]

currently lacks:

- rigorous definition
- uniqueness guarantees
- invariant characterization
- asymptotic proofs
- computational complexity analysis

A complete basin classification theory does not yet exist.

---

# 2. Torsion Vector Formalization

The object:

\[
\vec{\tau}(x)
\]

remains incompletely defined.

Open issues include:

- exact mathematical construction
- geometric meaning
- algebraic interpretation
- invariant properties
- recurrence characterization

---

# 3. Orbital Invariant Theory

Potential asymptotic invariants remain poorly understood.

Open questions include:

- cycle invariants
