# Research Methodology

## Overview

This document outlines the general research methodology used within the MDST and Yupana CRT framework.

The methodology emphasizes exploratory mathematical investigation combined with scientific caution and incremental formalization.

---

# 1. Foundational Principle

The framework treats modular dynamical systems as objects for exploratory investigation rather than immediately assuming practical computational usefulness.

The primary goal is conceptual and mathematical exploration.

---

# 2. Iterative Formalization

Research proceeds through repeated cycles of:

1. conceptual interpretation
2. mathematical abstraction
3. dynamical modeling
4. structural formalization
5. computational evaluation
6. critical revision

Many concepts remain provisional.

---

# 3. Functional Graph Analysis

A major methodological component involves studying:

\[
\mathcal{G}_{n,k}
\]

the functional graph induced by modular evolution.

The graph acts as a primary structural representation of recurrent dynamics.

---

# 4. CRT Decomposition Strategy

The framework relies heavily on CRT decomposition:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\cong
\prod_i \mathbb{Z}_{p_i^{\alpha_i}}
\]

to separate global dynamics into CRT-local recurrent domains.

This decomposition guides both theoretical and architectural investigation.

---

# 5. Asymptotic Analysis

Research emphasizes asymptotic structure including:

- recurrent attractors
- basin organization
- orbital recurrence
- cyclic dynamics
- long-term structural behavior

inside finite modular phase spaces.

---

# 6. Exploratory Geometric Interpretation

The framework intentionally experiments with geometric interpretations including:

- orbital manifolds
- toroidal phase structure
- recurrent geometries
- basin maps
- orbital flow fields

These interpretations remain heuristic unless rigorously formalized.

---

# 7. Hardware Speculation Discipline

Hardware interpretations are treated as speculative architectural hypotheses rather than engineering conclusions.

Potential mappings to:

- FPGA fabrics
- systolic arrays
- distributed SRAM systems
- modular execution fabrics

require independent validation.

---

# 8. Scientific Prudence

The methodology explicitly avoids claiming:

- computational superiority
- revolutionary performance
- AI breakthroughs
- industrial replacement capability

without rigorous evidence.

Negative results remain scientifically acceptable.

---

# 9. Formalization Priority

Priority areas for future formalization include:

- invariant theory
- torsion vector definition
- basin classification
- spectral structure
- asymptotic equivalence
- computational semantics

---

# 10. Experimental Philosophy

The framework encourages:

- reproducible experimentation
- graph visualization
- orbital simulation
- recurrence analysis
- asymptotic classification testing

as mechanisms for exploratory investigation.

---

# 11. Validation Requirements

Meaningful scientific advancement requires:

- formal proofs
- peer review
- reproducible benchmarks
- hardware prototypes
- independent replication

The framework currently lacks most of these components.

---

# 12. Current Position

At present, the methodology primarily supports:

- exploratory mathematical synthesis
- speculative computational investigation
- conceptual finite dynamical systems research

with substantial scientific development remaining open.
