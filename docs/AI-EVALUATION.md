# AI Evaluation of MDST and Yupana CRT

## Overview

This document summarizes an analytical evaluation of the MDST and Yupana CRT framework from the perspective of current academic and scientific standards.

The purpose is not promotional. The goal is to distinguish clearly between:

- established mathematics
- original synthesis
- speculative interpretation
- potential research significance
- unresolved limitations

---

# 1. What Has Been Constructed

The framework currently consists of three interconnected conceptual artifacts.

---

## 1.1 MDST (Modular Dynamical Systems Toolkit)

A theoretical framework combining:

- CRT decomposition of finite rings
- modular dynamics over:
  
\[
f_k(x)=x^k \bmod n
\]

- functional graph structure
- attractor basin decomposition
- asymptotic classification through:

\[
\Phi(x)
\]

- finite orbital geometry
- torsional periodic structure

The framework attempts to reinterpret modular arithmetic as a structured finite dynamical geometry.

---

## 1.2 Yupana CRT

A computational interpretation of MDST emphasizing:

- CRT-local execution
- lookup-driven modular evolution
- spatial computation
- carry-free arithmetic
- distributed modular architectures
- FPGA-oriented execution concepts

The project investigates whether modular dynamical structure may serve as a computational substrate.

---

## 1.3 Modular Torsion

The framework introduces the torsion descriptor:

\[
\vec{\tau}(x)
\]

intended to characterize local orbital periodicity across CRT components.

The concept attempts to extend asymptotic classification beyond attractor membership toward finer recurrent structure.

Further mathematical rigor, implementation, and independent evaluation are essential.
