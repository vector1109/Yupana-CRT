# Terminology

## Overview

This document summarizes frequently used terminology within the MDST and Yupana CRT framework.

Many terms are exploratory conceptual constructs rather than formally established mathematical objects.

---

# 1. Modular Phase Space

The finite dynamical domain:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\]

interpreted as a recurrent orbital geometry generated through modular evolution.

---

# 2. Orbital Dynamics

Repeated application of a modular transformation such as:

\[
f_k(x)=x^k \bmod n
\]

producing finite-state trajectories inside modular phase space.

---

# 3. Orbital Trajectory

The sequence:

\[
x,\ f(x),\ f(f(x)),\dots
\]

generated through repeated modular evolution.

---

# 4. Recurrent Attractor

A periodic orbital structure eventually reached by modular trajectories.

Examples include:

- fixed points
- cyclic loops
- periodic recurrent regions

---

# 5. Basin

The set of states converging toward the same recurrent attractor organization.

Basins partition modular phase space into asymptotic sectors.

---

# 6. Basin Classification Operator

The operator:

\[
\Phi(x)
\]

which conceptually classifies states according to asymptotic basin organization.

Its formal definition remains incomplete.

---

# 7. Torsion Vector

The object:

\[
\vec{\tau}(x)
\]

which conceptually captures recurrent cyclic organization across CRT-local domains.

Its formal definition remains incomplete.

---

# 8. CRT Decomposition

The decomposition:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\cong
\prod_i \mathbb{Z}_{p_i^{\alpha_i}}
\]

used to separate global modular dynamics into CRT-local recurrent systems.

---

# 9. CRT-Local Domain

An individual modular coordinate domain:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_{p_i^{\alpha_i}}
\]

inside CRT decomposition.

---

# 10. Functional Graph

The directed graph:

\[
\mathcal{G}_{n,k}
\]

whose vertices are modular states and whose edges represent modular evolution.

---

# 11. Orbital Corridor

A transient pathway through modular phase space connecting recurrent regions.

---

# 12. Orbital Transition Zone

A region where trajectories move between distinct asymptotic organizational structures.

---

# 13. Toroidal Phase Structure

A heuristic geometric interpretation arising from cyclic CRT-local recurrence and wrapped orbital organization.

---

# 14. Orbital Flow

Directed asymptotic state evolution inside modular phase space.

---

# 15. Asymptotic Routing

A speculative computational interpretation where recurrent attractor organization guides state evolution.

---

# 16. Recurrent Modular Symmetry

A structural regularity preserved across recurrent orbital organization.

---

# 17. Orbital Recurrence Spectrum

A speculative spectral characterization of recurrent modular dynamics.

---

# 18. Modular Recurrence Engine

A speculative hardware interpretation based on recurrent modular orbital evolution.

---

# 19. Basin-Oriented Computation

A speculative computational interpretation using asymptotic attractor organization as a processing mechanism.

---

# 20. Scientific Status

Most terminology in this framework should currently be interpreted as:

- exploratory
- partially formalized
- conceptually motivated
- experimentally unvalidated

rather than established scientific vocabulary.
