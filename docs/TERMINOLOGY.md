# Terminology

## Overview

This document summarizes major conceptual terminology used throughout the MDST and Yupana CRT framework.

Most terms remain exploratory and should not automatically be interpreted as formally established mathematical objects.

---

# 1. Modular Dynamical System

A finite-state dynamical system generated through repeated modular transformations such as:

\[
f_k(x)=x^k \bmod n
\]

acting over:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\]

---

# 2. Orbital Dynamics

The directed evolution of modular states under repeated iteration:

\[
x \to f_k(x)
\]

producing recurrent trajectories inside finite modular phase space.

---

# 3. Basin

A recurrent asymptotic sector whose trajectories eventually converge toward the same attractor or recurrent cycle.

---

# 4. Attractor

A periodic recurrent cycle reached asymptotically through repeated modular iteration.

---

# 5. Transient Tree

A directed orbital structure feeding into recurrent cyclic attractors inside the functional graph.

---

# 6. Functional Graph

The directed graph:

\[
\mathcal{G}_{n,k}
\]

whose vertices represent modular states and whose edges represent directed orbital evolution.

---

# 7. CRT-Local Domain

A modular subdomain generated through CRT decomposition:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\cong
\prod_i \mathbb{Z}_{p_i^{\alpha_i}}
\]

where each coordinate evolves independently algebraically.

---

# 8. Basin Classification Operator

The conceptual operator:

\[
\Phi(x)
\]

intended to classify asymptotic basin organization.

Precise formalization remains incomplete.

---

# 9. Torsion Vector

The conceptual object:

\[
\vec{\tau}(x)
\]

intended to capture recurrent cyclic organization across CRT-local domains.

Precise mathematical definition remains unresolved.

---

# 10. Orbital Synchronization

Potential coordinated recurrent behavior across distributed cyclic structures inside modular phase space.

Currently heuristic.

---

# 11. Recurrent Phase Landscape

A conceptual large-scale organizational structure generated through asymptotic recurrence across modular phase space.

---

# 12. Orbital Recurrence Field

A conceptual field-like organizational interpretation of recurrent asymptotic flow inside modular dynamical systems.

---

# 13. Basin Transition Manifold

A conceptual transition structure between recurrent asymptotic basin regions.

Formal manifold structure remains undefined.

---

# 14. Orbital Routing

A speculative computational interpretation in which directed orbital propagation acts as a routing mechanism across recurrent modular regions.

---

# 15. Recurrent Modular Fabric

A speculative distributed computational architecture organized through recurrent modular orbital dynamics.

---

# 16. Orbital Processor

A speculative processor-like architecture organized around recurrent orbital execution dynamics.

No validated implementation currently exists.

---

# 17. Torsional Synchronization

Potential cyclic coherence emerging across distributed recurrent modular domains.

Currently heuristic.

---

# 18. Scientific Status

Most terminology within the framework should currently be interpreted as:

- exploratory
- partially formalized
- conceptually motivated
- experimentally unvalidated
- subject to revision

rather than scientifically established terminology.
