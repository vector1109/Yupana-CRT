# Yupana CRT — Scientific Positioning

## Purpose

This document clarifies the intended scientific and technological positioning of the Yupana CRT project.

---

## What Yupana CRT Is

Yupana CRT is:

- an exploratory computational architecture
- a CRT-based modular execution framework
- a finite dynamical systems research project
- a theoretical investigation into non-iterative modular computation

The project explores whether modular dynamical geometry can act as a computational substrate.

---

## What Yupana CRT Is NOT

Yupana CRT is NOT:

- a replacement for classical computing
- a quantum computing alternative
- a proven high-performance architecture
- a demonstrated AI breakthrough
- a commercially validated processor design

No such claims are currently made.

---

## Relationship to Existing Fields

The project intersects with:

- finite dynamical systems
- computational number theory
- residue number systems (RNS)
- functional graph theory
- FPGA architectures
- discrete geometry

However, the combination of:

- Phi-based attractor classification
- CRT-local execution
- modular torsion dynamics
- non-iterative asymptotic inference

appears to define a distinct research direction.

---

## Core Novelty Claims

The project currently claims originality in:

### Phi Classification

A direct algebraic attractor classifier without iterative simulation.

---

### Modular Torsion

A computational interpretation of periodic orbital structure as a dynamical degree of freedom.

---

### Unified CRT Dynamical Architecture

A framework combining:

- finite-ring dynamics
- attractor geometry
- torsion structure
- hardware-oriented execution semantics

within a single computational model.

---

## Current Maturity Level

The repository currently contains:

- theoretical definitions
- architectural concepts
- exploratory specifications
- foundational documentation

It does not yet contain:

- validated hardware
- large-scale benchmarks
- industrial implementations
- peer-reviewed publications

---

## Long-Term Direction

Potential future directions include:

- FPGA prototypes
- modular neural systems
- torsion-aware inference engines
- finite dynamical hardware accelerators
- algebraic execution fabrics

---

## Research Philosophy

The project prioritizes:

- mathematical rigor
- architectural clarity
- explicit limitations
- separation between theory and speculation

Exploratory ideas are identified as such.
