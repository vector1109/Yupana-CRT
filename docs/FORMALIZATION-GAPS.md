# Formalization Gaps

## Overview

This document summarizes major formalization gaps currently present within the MDST and Yupana CRT framework.

Most concepts remain partially formalized and should currently be interpreted as exploratory research constructs.

---

# 1. Basin Classification Formalization

The operator:

\[
\Phi(x)
\]

currently lacks:

- rigorous construction
- uniqueness guarantees
- invariant characterization
- complexity analysis
- asymptotic proofs

The notion of basin equivalence remains partially heuristic.

---

- algebraic characterization
