# CRT Dynamical Decomposition

## Overview

This document describes CRT dynamical decomposition within the MDST and Yupana CRT framework.

The central idea is that modular dynamical systems over:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\]

can be factorized into independent local dynamical systems through the Chinese Remainder Theorem.

---

# 1. Modular Dynamical System

Consider the modular map:

\[
f_k(x)=x^k \bmod n
\]

acting over the finite ring:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\]

---

# 2. CRT Factorization

Let:

\[
n=\prod_i p_i^{\alpha_i}
\]

Then:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\cong
\prod_i \mathbb{Z}_{p_i^{\alpha_i}}
\]

This decomposition transforms the global system into independent local modular domains.

---

# 3. State Decomposition

Each state:

\[
x
\]

admits CRT coordinates:

\[
x
\leftrightarrow
(x_1,x_2,\dots,x_r)
\]

where:

\[
x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{p_i^{\alpha_i}}
\]

---

# 4. Local Dynamical Evolution

The global transformation factorizes into local maps:

\[
f_k(x_1,\dots,x_r)
=
(x_1^k,\dots,x_r^k)
\]

Each CRT coordinate evolves independently.

---

# 5. Independent Orbital Structure

Local systems generate independent:

- periodic cycles
- attractor basins
- transient trees
- torsional structures

The global orbit emerges from their product interaction.

---

# 6. Basin Decomposition

The operator:

\[
\Phi(x)
\]

classifies asymptotic structure through local CRT evaluation.

Global basin structure therefore becomes a product of local asymptotic sectors.

---

# 7. Torsional Composition

The torsion vector:

\[
\vec{\tau}(x)
\]

captures local orbital periodicities across CRT components.

Global recurrent behavior emerges from combined local cycle structure.

---
