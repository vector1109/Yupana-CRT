# Torsion Dynamics

## Overview

This document describes the concept of torsion dynamics within the MDST and Yupana CRT framework.

Torsion refers to periodic orbital structure emerging inside modular dynamical systems over finite rings.

---

# 1. Dynamical Context

Consider the modular map:

\[
f_k(x)=x^k \bmod n
\]

acting over:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\]

Because the state space is finite, every orbit eventually becomes periodic.

---

Certain states evolve into cycles:

\[

\to

\to
\dots
\to
x
\]

These periodic structures define torsional behavior.

# 3. Local CRT Cycles

Under CRT decomposition:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n

The global orbit emerges from the product of local cycles.
