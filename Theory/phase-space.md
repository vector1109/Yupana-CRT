# Modular Phase Space

## Overview

Yupana CRT interprets modular arithmetic systems as finite dynamical phase spaces.

Under this interpretation, computation becomes movement across structured algebraic geometries rather than sequential symbolic reduction alone.

---

## State Space Definition

Given:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\]

the complete modular state space contains all residue classes modulo \(n\).

Through CRT decomposition:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\cong
\prod_i \mathbb{Z}_{p_i^{\alpha_i}}
\]

the phase space becomes a Cartesian product of independent modular regions.

---

## Dynamical Evolution

The map:

\[
f_k(x)=x^k \bmod n
\]

induces trajectories over the phase space.

Repeated evolution generates:

- transient flows
- attractor basins

- torsional regions

inside the modular geometry.

## Attractor Structure

The operator:

Each region represents a basin of dynamical convergence.

This creates a coarse geometric classification of the modular landscape.
