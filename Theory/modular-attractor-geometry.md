# Modular Attractor Geometry

## Overview

This document describes the geometric interpretation of attractor structures within modular dynamical systems.

The MDST and Yupana CRT framework treats asymptotic modular behavior as a structured finite geometry rather than merely iterative arithmetic evolution.

---

# 1. Modular Dynamical Systems

Consider:

\[
f_k(x)=x^k \bmod n
\]

acting over:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\]

Because the space is finite, every orbit eventually enters a periodic or fixed asymptotic structure.

---

# 2. Attractors in Finite Rings

Within finite modular systems, attractors emerge naturally through repeated dynamical evolution.

These include:

- fixed points
- idempotent sinks
- periodic cycles
- torsional structures

inside the modular phase space.

---

# 3. CRT Factorization

CRT decomposition yields:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\cong
\prod_i \mathbb{Z}_{p_i^{\alpha_i}}
\]

The global attractor geometry becomes the product of local modular geometries.

inside the modular geometry.
