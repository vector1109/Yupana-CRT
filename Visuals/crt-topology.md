# CRT Topology and Spatial Geometry

## Overview

Yupana CRT represents modular computation as a spatially decomposed topology generated through CRT factorization.

Instead of viewing computation as sequential symbolic manipulation, the system interprets state evolution as movement inside a discrete geometric structure.

---

## CRT Spatial Decomposition

Given:

\[
n=\prod_i p_i^{\alpha_i}
\]

the state space decomposes as:

\[
\mathbb{Z}_n
\cong
\prod_i \mathbb{Z}_{p_i^{\alpha_i}}
\]

Each coordinate acts as an independent spatial axis.

---

## Toroidal Interpretation

The Cartesian product of modular cyclic spaces induces a toroidal discrete geometry.

Each component wraps cyclically:

\[
x_i \rightarrow x_i + 1 \bmod p_i^{\alpha_i}
\]

creating periodic spatial dimensions.

---

## Dynamical Geometry

The function:

\[
f_k(x)=x^k \bmod n
\]

induces trajectories over the CRT torus.

These trajectories form:

- attractor basins
- transient trees
- periodic cycles
- torsional regions

inside the modular geometry.

---

## Basin Partitioning

The attractor classifier:

\[
\Phi(x)
\]

partitions the torus into discrete dynamical regions.

Each region corresponds to:

- an asymptotic attractor
- a stability domain
- a dynamical equivalence class
