# Contributing to Yupana CRT

## Overview

Yupana CRT is an exploratory research repository focused on:

- CRT-based computation
- modular dynamical systems
- torsion-aware execution
- finite-state computational architectures

Contributions are welcome in theoretical, computational, visualization, and hardware-related areas.

---

## Contribution Areas

### Theory

- finite ring dynamics
- CRT decomposition methods
- torsion formalization
- functional graph analysis
- discrete dynamical systems

---

### Hardware

- FPGA experiments
- modular execution fabrics
- ASIC feasibility studies
- parallel scheduling systems

---

### Software

- CRT execution engines
- modular simulation frameworks
- visualization systems
- attractor analysis tools

---

### Documentation

- mathematical clarification
- examples
- diagrams
- benchmarks
- literature references

---

## Guidelines

### Maintain Formal Clarity

Use mathematically precise terminology whenever possible.

---

### Preserve Separation of Layers

Keep distinctions clear between:

- MDST theory
- Yupana CRT architecture
- speculative future applications

---

### Avoid Unsupported Claims

Do not claim:

- computational supremacy
- replacement of classical systems
- practical superiority

unless experimentally demonstrated.

---

## Research Nature

This repository is exploratory and research-oriented.

Some concepts may evolve significantly over time.
